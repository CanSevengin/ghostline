# Writing rules

Every note must pass all of these before it is published.

## Honesty

- Every factual claim is checked against a source. Unverifiable claims are cut.
- No invented statistics, quotes, clients, stories or personal experiences.
- Only biography from `profile.yml` is used.
- Notes are disclosed as drafted with Claude (the site template shows this under every post).

## Voice

- First person, plain language, short paragraphs.
- Opinionated but fair. Say what you think, and why.
- No hype words. No sales pitch.
- No em dashes or en dashes between clauses. Use commas, colons or full stops.

## Shape

- 600-900 words.
- The core question is answered in the first 2-3 sentences.
- A few `##` headings that read like answers, not labels.
- 1-2 internal links to earlier notes where they help.
- A "Sources" list at the end when news or data is cited.

## SEO and AI search

- One clear search intent per note.
- Main phrase in the title, description, first paragraph and one heading.
- Description: 140-160 characters, written as a direct answer.
- Slug: short, kebab-case, contains the main phrase.
